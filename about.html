Our story

Empowering students and transforming lives since 1998.

Sha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain Arts, Commerce and Science Pre-University College is dedicated to accessible, high-quality education and clear career direction. More than 38,000 students have graduated from the college and built careers across public service, technology, healthcare, finance, business and agriculture.

Our approach combines subject mastery, integrated coaching, close mentorship, strong values and opportunities beyond the classroom.
