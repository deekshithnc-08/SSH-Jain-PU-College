Frequently asked questions

Useful information before you visit.

Which streams are available?

Science PCMB, Commerce CEBA, and two Arts combinations: HEPOK and HPEOK.

Is integrated coaching available?

Yes. Coaching options include JEE, NEET, CET and NDA for Science; CA, CPT, CS and Banking for Commerce; and selected government-exam preparation for Arts.

Where can I find the current fees?

The college office confirms the approved fee based on the stream and selected coaching pathway.

Can I apply online?

Yes. Use the admission enquiry form and provide a valid parent or guardian contact number.
