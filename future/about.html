Our story

28 years of opening doors.

Established in 1999, Sha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain Arts, Commerce and Science Pre-University College has remained committed to quality education, disciplined learning and reliable guidance.

More than 38,000 graduates have moved from our classrooms into higher education, public service, healthcare, engineering, finance, business and agriculture.
