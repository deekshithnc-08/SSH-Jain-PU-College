From the desk of the Chairman

Shaping Futures, Empowering Minds

Welcome to SHA SHESHAJI HASTHIMAL JAIN PU College.

When we established this institution, our vision was clear: to create an educational ecosystem where academic excellence meets strong moral values. Pre-University education is the critical bridge between childhood learning and professional careers.

Whether a student chooses Science, Commerce or Arts, we ensure a strong foundation through laboratories, libraries and experienced faculty. Our goal is to nurture compassionate leaders, innovative thinkers and responsible global citizens.

ChairmanSSH Jain PU College · V.V. Sangha, Ballari

From the desk of the Principal

Learning with Discipline and Direction

Dear Students and Parents, welcome to Sha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain PU College, Harapanahalli.

For the past 28 years, our college has provided quality education to students in our region. Our highly experienced faculty helps every learner understand subjects clearly and succeed.

Alongside regular classes, we offer entrance coaching for Science, professional coaching for Commerce and competitive-exam preparation for Arts. We also value discipline, sports, cultural events and skill development.

Dr. Sharath A. M.Principal · D.Com., B.Ed., M.A., Ph.D.
