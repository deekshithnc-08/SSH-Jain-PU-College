From the desk of the Chairman

Shaping Futures, Empowering Minds

Welcome to Sha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain PU College.

When we established this institution, our vision was clear: to create an educational ecosystem where academic excellence meets strong moral values. Pre-University education is the critical bridge between childhood learning and professional careers. It is during these two transformative years that young minds discover their true potential and carve out their paths for the future.

Whether a student chooses the analytical rigour of Science, the strategic dynamics of Commerce, or the creative and critical depth of Arts, we ensure they receive a strong foundation. Our campus is built to foster curiosity, discipline and holistic growth through laboratories, library resources and experienced faculty.

We do not aim only for academic ranks. Our goal is to nurture compassionate leaders, innovative thinkers and responsible citizens. I invite you to join our community and take the first step toward a bright and impactful future.

ChairmanSha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain PU College · V.V. Sangha, Ballari

From the desk of the Principal

Learning with Discipline and Direction

Dear Students and Parents, welcome to Sha Sheshaji Hasthimal Jain PU College, Harapanahalli.

For the past 28 years, our college has provided quality education to students in our region. Our greatest strength is our highly experienced teaching faculty. With their knowledge and guidance, they help every student understand subjects clearly and succeed in their studies.

The Pre-University years are important for planning a future career. Alongside regular classes, we offer integrated coaching: CET, JEE and NEET for Science; CA, CPT, CS and Banking for Commerce; and preparation for KAS, VAO, PDO, SDA, FDA, Police, Forest Guard and SI examinations for Arts.

Education is not only about books and marks. We give great importance to discipline, character, sports, cultural events and skill development so students grow into confident and capable individuals.

Dr. Sharath A. M.Principal · D.Com., B.Ed., M.A., Ph.D.
